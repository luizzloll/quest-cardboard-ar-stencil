Cardboard AR Stencil

A real-size outline that floats over cardboard on your table. Align it with a controller, trace with a blunt marker, then remove your headset before cutting.

Controls in passthrough

  • Controller trigger / hand pinch: point at your cardboard and place or move stencil
  • Controller thumbstick left/right: rotate the outline
  • Thumbstick up/down: raise/lower stencil in 1 cm increments
  • Grip/squeeze: touch the controller to the tabletop and sample its height
  • A / X button: flip template for other side

Keep the template away from real cutting tools. Small passthrough positioning errors are normal. Use a ruler to verify 80 mm before tracing.
